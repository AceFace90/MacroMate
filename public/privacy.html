MacroMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign in (with Apple or Google), your email address and name as provided by that service.
  • Food log and profile: foods you log, and profile details you enter (e.g. age, height, weight, goals). If you sign in, these sync to our database (Supabase). If you don't, they stay on your device.
  • Apple Health (optional): with your permission, MacroMate writes daily calories, protein, carbs and fat to Apple Health and reads your body weight. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Photos and text for AI logging (optional): processed on-device with Apple Intelligence or Apple Private Cloud Compute. Only if you add your own Gemini API key are the text or photos you analyse sent to Google's Gemini service, using your key.
  • Barcode lookups: the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, and we do not sell your data.

Retention and deletion

Synced data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account and all synced data at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. You can turn off Apple Health access in Settings at any time and revoke it in the Health app. Local data is removed by deleting the app.

Third parties

Supabase (hosting and authentication), Apple and Google (sign-in), Open Food Facts (barcode lookups), and Google Gemini (only if you add a key).

Not medical advice

Nutritional values are estimates and are not intended for medical use.
